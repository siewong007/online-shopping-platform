# Ekoway Storefront Redesign Round 2 — Creative Brief

Date: 2026-08-02  
Owner: request author  
Gate 0: **Approved in the current task**  
Delegation: the owner supplied the target feelings, rejection risks, and retained brand cue;
reference selection, boldness, and signature development were delegated to the design agent.

## Current status

- Step 5 Pass 2 is a functional prototype whose working behaviour must be preserved.
- Its visual direction is rejected: boring, dated, flat, generic, administratively styled,
  and particularly weak in mobile product composition.
- Pass 3 remains blocked.

## Approved brief

- **Business outcome:** help Sibu homeowners and trade buyers find, compare, and open real
  products quickly in a storefront that feels premium, innovative, and professional.
- **First-five-second feelings:** premium, innovative, professional.
- **Boldness:** 4/5 — a clear visual departure, while product facts and buying actions stay
  immediately understandable.
- **Retained brand cue:** Ekoway logo only. Palette, typography, composition, imagery
  treatment, and motion may diverge.
- **Signature:** **Ekoway Counter Rail** — one tactile category rail immediately below the
  header, with material-like markers and a precise active-position indicator. It is the only
  category navigation on desktop and mobile.

## Reference principles

| Reference | Learn | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| Festool | technical authority, system thinking, confident tool presentation | deep navigation or its corporate palette |
| Bang & Olufsen | premium art direction, material detail, typography, restraint | sparse luxury layouts that delay product discovery |
| Material Bank | tactile material-led discovery and coherent filtering | enterprise workflows, project boards, or new business features |

## Anti-goals

- Administrative catalogue or spreadsheet appearance.
- Flat white panels or a generic equal-card grid without art direction.
- An empty hero that delays product discovery.
- Two navigation systems or two category lists on the same page.
- Inconsistent price formatting or hierarchy between views.
- Fragmented mobile products whose image, name, price, stock, and action do not read as one
  deliberate composition.
- Novelty that obstructs search, comparison, product opening, or Add to Cart.

sorting, product opening, Add to Cart entry, translation structure, keyboard accessibility,
automated tests, and isolation of Admin, Cart, Account, Login, Checkout, and Payment.
