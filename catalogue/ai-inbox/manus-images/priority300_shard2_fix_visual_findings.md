# Priority-300 shard 0051–0100 visual fix findings

The official Khind FF2005 asset `https://khind.com.my/cdn/shop/files/ssmy.zone-1731060431-FF2005_WH.jpg?v=1751808994&width=1946` was visually inspected. Despite the legacy `_WH` filename, the rendered product is a black 20-inch industrial floor fan with black blades, grille, hub, and base, matching the source row’s black finish. The official Khind page separately confirms model FF2005, 20-inch size, black colour, 150 W, and 220–240 V.

The official Sorento 2024 catalogue PDF page 94 was visually inspected. It contains the SRTKS2406, SRTKS2426, SRTKS57040, SRTKS57030, SRTKS2409, and SRTKS2429 families, but not SRTKS2431. Therefore page 94 must not be used as the fix evidence for SRTKS2431; continued official Sorento page/PDF search is required, and the dealer asset remains rejected.
